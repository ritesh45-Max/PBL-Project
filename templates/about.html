About TruthGuard AI

AI-Based Fake News & Deepfake Detection Platform

Problem Statement

Misleading news, AI-generated images and manipulated videos spread quickly on social media. Most people do not have the time or tools to check each piece of content before they share it.

Our Solution

TruthGuard AI gives one simple place to analyse text, images and videos. It returns an authenticity score, explains the reasons behind it and recommends how to verify the content through trusted sources.

Project Objectives

  • Detect suspicious or misleading news text.
  • Identify possible AI-generated or manipulated images.
  • Flag possible deepfake patterns in videos.
  • Explain every result in plain language.
  • Teach users how to verify information before sharing.

Technology Used

  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Bootstrap 5
  • Backend: Python, Flask
  • AI / Image / Video: Python, Pillow, OpenCV (optional trained model hook)
  • Database: SQLite

How the Platform Works

Content is uploaded, prepared (text, metadata or video frames), checked by the detection module, converted into an authenticity score from 0 to 100, and shown on a results dashboard with reasons and recommendations.

The current build runs in Demo Analysis Mode unless a trained model is added. Demo checks are transparent heuristics, not genuine AI detection, and the results say so.
